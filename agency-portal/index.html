Apply for an agency officer account

For officers of an authorised Kenyan anti-corruption, investigative, prosecutorial or oversight institution (for example the EACC, DCI, ODPP, IPOA, the Auditor-General or the Ombudsman). Kenya Watch grants access only after an Admin confirms — through an official channel with your institution — who you are, that you are employed there, and that you are legally authorised to receive and handle corruption case referrals.

Agency Case Officer workspace

This portal is separate from Kenya Watch's internal Reviewer Queue. You can work only on referrals sent to your assigned agency. Each case shows the referral brief and location, plus the complaint narrative and evidence files only where a Kenya Watch reviewer has released them for your investigation. Opening a released case file or downloading evidence is recorded against your account. The reporter's identity is never shown; if you need more, ask Kenya Watch below. Optional messages for reporters are reviewed by a Kenya Watch Admin before sharing.

Your decisions carry the weight of the law. Once you acknowledge a case it is yours: a Kenya Watch reviewer cannot resolve it over you, withdraw it, or take released material back. Only an Admin can, and every such step is recorded. If a case is outside your institution's mandate, use return to Kenya Watch with your reason and it will be re-routed.
